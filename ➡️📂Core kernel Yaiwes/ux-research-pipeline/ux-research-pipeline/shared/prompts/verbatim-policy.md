# Verbatim policy (inserted into the prompt)

Any quote you place in quotation marks must:

1. **Exist in the transcript word-for-word.** Before inserting it, check it against the source. If you can't find it verbatim, do NOT put it in quotation marks. Paraphrase without quotes.
2. **Be anchored to a timecode** in `[mm:ss]` or `[hh:mm:ss]` format (if the interview is longer than 1 hour).
3. **Carry a respondent identifier** (`R0X`).
4. **Not be spliced** from utterances at different moments of the same interview. A quote is a continuous stretch of speech.

If the respondent_id or timecode is unknown (for example, in materials from an external agency with no timecodes), flag it explicitly: "quote without timecode, source: agency-transcript".

A precise, short paraphrase is always better than a "polished" quote.
