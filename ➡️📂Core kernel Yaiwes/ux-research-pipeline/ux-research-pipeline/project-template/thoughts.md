# Thoughts

> A free-form file. Put your observations, intuitions, and half-formed hypotheses about the project here. The agent reads this and factors it into the analysis and when assembling the report.

No need to structure it. A single line works, or a paragraph. With a timecode or without.

## Examples of what's useful to write down

- "On the third interview I got the sense that people talk about onboarding somewhat nervously — worth checking"
- "This bit from [[respondents/R03]] contradicts [[respondents/R07]] — look into it"
- "The stakeholder said at the start that conversion is what matters most. But the interviews suggest retention matters more. Factor into the recommendations"
- "The projective technique didn't work; replace it in the next interview guide"

## What does NOT belong here

- Quotes — they're already in `2-interviews/` and in the respondent cards in `3-analysis/respondents/`.
- Coded segments — they're in `.system/coded/`, the agent reads them itself.
- System metadata — that's in `.system/runs/`.
