# 2-interviews / inscriptions

Put **everything that isn't the interview recording itself** here, but that was produced before, during, or right after it:

- **Field notebook / interviewer notes** — whatever you jotted down as you went. Any format: a photo of the notebook, .md, .txt, .docx, .pdf.
- **Debrief protocol** — a short "right after the interview" note from your partner (or your own, if you ran it solo). Hypotheses, surprising moments, things to check next.
- **Sample log / recruiting request** — who you tried to invite, who agreed, who declined and why. If you have it.
- **Screenshots / artifacts from the respondent** — what the respondent showed on screen, their sketches, screenshots from their product.

This isn't raw material for coding, but it's **important context** during analysis. For example, in the debrief you might have written "R03 visibly tensed up when we asked about X" — that won't be in the audio transcript, but in axial coding it changes the interpretation.

## Filename

A handy convention: `<respondent ID>-<type>.<ext>`, for example:
- `R03-notes.md`
- `R03-debrief.txt`
- `R07-screenshot-1.png`

If a file covers the whole project (for example, a sample log for all respondents), name it by meaning: `sample-log.xlsx`.

## What will happen

When interview files first appear in `../` (or, if you put notes here straight away, the first time it touches them), the agent will **always** ask what other context exists and pull the inscriptions into coding and analysis. Without an explicit "no, that's everything" it won't move on.

## What NOT to put here

- The transcripts themselves (those are in `../`).
- Audio/video files (those are in `../`).
- Final artifacts like the report (those are in `4-output/`).
- Respondent PII in the clear — name/phone/email. Use the ID `R0X` and aggregated demographics.

## NDA

All contents are confidential. They are not committed to git (see the plugin's `.gitignore`).
