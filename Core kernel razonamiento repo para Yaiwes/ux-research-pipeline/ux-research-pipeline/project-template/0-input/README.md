# 0-input

Drop everything the stakeholder brings, and everything you have before the start, here.

## What to put here

- The stakeholder kickoff recording (mp3 / mp4 / m4a / wav / mov). The agent transcribes it and drafts a brief in `1-methodology/`.
- Notes from the stakeholder (md / txt / docx / pdf).
- Any materials on the topic: links to documents, screenshots, diagrams.

## What will happen

Once you've dropped a kickoff recording here:
1. The agent transcribes it (a `<name>.txt` appears next to it).
2. It analyzes the meeting: what the stakeholder wants, what constraints exist, what hypotheses they already hold.
3. It puts a draft brief in `1-methodology/brief.md`.
4. It proposes 2–3 research design options (generative/evaluative, method, sample).

In chat — a short summary, no skill names and no JSON.

## What NOT to put here

- Interview recordings — those go in `2-interviews/`.
- Your final reports — that's `4-output/`.
