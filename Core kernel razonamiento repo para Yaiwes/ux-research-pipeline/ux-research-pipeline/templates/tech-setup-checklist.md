# Tech setup checklist — before the interview

> This is not an agent skill. It's your checklist before you start recording.

## The day before the interview

- [ ] The respondent received the invitation, link, and instructions.
- [ ] Confirmed the time.
- [ ] If screen-share is needed — verified the respondent has the right device and is comfortable with it.

## An hour before

- [ ] Charged the laptop to 100% (or plug it in).
- [ ] Opened the guide (`1-methodology/guide.md`) on a side monitor.
- [ ] Opened a notes document: format `2-interviews/R0X-notes.md` (create it before the interview, keep it open).
- [ ] Checked the microphone: a 30-second test recording, played it back.
- [ ] Closed everything unnecessary: Slack/Telegram/messengers, so they don't distract you.

## Five minutes before

- [ ] Opened the video call app.
- [ ] **Started recording** (important — hit it ahead of time, not after "hello").
- [ ] If there's a screenshare: verified the recording captures the screen.
- [ ] Opened a copy of the guide — somewhere visible.
- [ ] A glass of water.

## After the interview

- [ ] Saved the recording locally.
- [ ] Placed it in `2-interviews/R0X.<ext>`.
- [ ] Placed `R0X-notes.md` next to it (if you took notes).
- [ ] If the respondent shared screenshots — placed them in `2-interviews/R0X-screens/`.
- [ ] Noted the main impressions in `thoughts.md` (1–3 lines) — while it's fresh.

## After three interviews

- [ ] Interim debrief: run `10-saturation-map`, look at `3-analysis/_index.md`.
- [ ] If the guide needs adjustments — discuss with the team and update `1-methodology/guide.md`. The previous version is preserved in git.

## What you should NOT do

- Do NOT edit the recording (trim, splice) — its integrity is needed for diarization.
- Do NOT rename the respondent ID after the start — it will break mappings.
- Do NOT pass the recording through messengers — only locally and through corporate storage.
