## 0. Intro and consent (~3 min)
- Introduce yourself, briefly state the purpose of the interview (without revealing hypotheses).
- Consent to recording: "everything you say stays within the team; we use it for design decisions; you can stop at any time."
- Warm-up question: "tell me a little about yourself and how you usually...".

## Wording audit

| Question | Issue |
|---|---|
| "Is X convenient for you to use?" | leading — presupposes "convenient." Replace with "tell me how you use X. What was easy about it, what was hard?" |
| "Is it clear and convenient to you?" | double-barreled. Split into two. |

## Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] Every RQ is covered by at least one block
- [ ] Every block maps to an RQ
- [ ] No leading / double-barreled questions (or they've been fixed)
- [ ] Timing is realistic (sum ≤ the stated minutes)
- [ ] There are probe questions in every block (not just open ones)
- [ ] The wrap-up includes "what did we forget to ask"
