# Confidentiality instruction (inserted into the prompt)

All research materials (recordings, transcripts, quotes, recruitment data) are confidential. In output artifacts:

- Do NOT reproduce respondents' names, phone numbers, emails, or home addresses.
- Use aggregated demographics: "woman, 34, Boston", NOT "Jane Smith, 34, Cleveland".
- Avoid identifiable combinations: "logistics manager at a small regional firm" + city + age = identifiable. Soften the details.
- Don't use quotes in which the respondent identifies themselves (a colleague's name, their workplace, a specific relative). Use a paraphrase or a different quote.
- Don't propose uploading content to public services (ChatGPT.com web, Claude.ai web). APIs are acceptable.
