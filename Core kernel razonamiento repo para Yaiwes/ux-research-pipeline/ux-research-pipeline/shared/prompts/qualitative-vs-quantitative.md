# Qualitative vs quantitative conclusions (inserted into the prompt)

This is a **qualitative** study, typically with a sample of 8–15 respondents. Do not use:

- Percentages: "72% said..." — not allowed.
- Numbers framed as proportions: instead of "more than half", say "most of them" / "a few of them".
- Claims of statistical significance: "significantly more often" — no, our sample doesn't support that.

Use phrasings like:

- "most of them" / "a few of them" / "one or two".
- "a recurring pattern" / "a one-off observation".
- "needs quantitative verification" — for cases where a pattern is interesting but you need the numbers.

If the data is interesting as counts, that's a signal you need a follow-up study (a tracking survey, a poll). Note this in the conclusions.
